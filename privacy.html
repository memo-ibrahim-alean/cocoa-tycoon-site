Privacy Policy

Cocoa Tycoon by Mo Games · Effective October 23, 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Cocoa Tycoon ("the game") handles. Mo Games ("we") made the game. Contact: mohamed.ibrahim.alean@gmail.com.

Information we do not collect

The game has no accounts and no sign-in. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location, and we do not run our own servers that receive data from the game.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (money, unlocked items, country, language and sound settings) is saved only on your device. It is deleted when you uninstall the game.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows optional rewarded video ads through Google AdMob. When an ad is requested or shown, Google may collect and process:

  • device identifiers such as the advertising ID (Android) or IDFA (iOS, only if you allow tracking);
  • IP address and the approximate location it implies;
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version);
  • ad interactions (which ads were shown or watched) and diagnostic data.

Google uses this information to deliver, measure and personalize ads and to prevent fraud. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your choices

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, using Google's consent tool.
  • On iOS, the game only uses your IDFA if you allow tracking in the system prompt. You can change this any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads).
  • Watching ads is always optional.

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and its effective date.
